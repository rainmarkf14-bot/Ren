Our Story

About Montalban Peak Academy

Montalban Peak Academy is a proposed private Senior High School to be established in Montalban (Rodriguez), Rizal. The school aims to provide accessible, quality, and relevant education for Grade 11 and Grade 12 learners.

The school emphasizes academic excellence, practical skills, character development, creativity, and responsible technology use.

Montalban Peak Academy envisions becoming a leading private Senior High School that develops competent, responsible, and purpose-driven learners who are equipped with the knowledge, skills, values, and adaptability needed to succeed in higher education, employment, entrepreneurship, and community life.
Montalban Peak Academy is committed to providing quality, inclusive, and relevant Senior High School education through meaningful learning experiences, practical skills development, technology integration, and character formation.

Montalban Peak Academy follows a learner-centered approach grounded in Progressivism, which values experience and real-world relevance; Constructivism, which encourages learners to build understanding through inquiry and reflection; and selected elements of Essentialism, which ensures learners acquire core academic knowledge and skills. Together, these philosophies shape a curriculum that is practical, values-driven, and responsive to the needs of every learner.
